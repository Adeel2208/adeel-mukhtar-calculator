# Jobs To Be Done

Each job describes what the person needs to get done whether or not FairShare exists. None of them name a screen, button or feature. Roles are defined in [app-roles.md](app-roles.md).

| Job | Role | Short name |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| J-1 | R-1 Bill Payer | Get my money back exactly |
| J-2 | R-1 Bill Payer | Agree on a tip quickly |
| J-3 | R-2 Itemizing Diner | Pay for what I ordered |
| J-4 | R-2 Itemizing Diner | Split shared dishes among the people who ate them |
| J-5 | R-1 Bill Payer | Ask for money in a way people trust |
| J-6 | R-1 Bill Payer | Collect cash without small change |

---

### J-1 Get my money back exactly (Bill Payer)

> When I have paid a group bill with my own card, I want to know exactly how much each person owes me, so I can get all of my money back without overcharging anyone.

### J-2 Agree on a tip quickly (Bill Payer)

> When the group has to agree on a tip, I want to see what each tip level would cost in total and per person, so I can help the table agree on a tip quickly and settle up.

### J-3 Pay for what I ordered (Itemizing Diner)

> When people at my table ordered very different amounts, I want to pay for what I ordered plus a fair part of the tax and tip, so I can avoid covering the cost of other people's more expensive orders.

### J-4 Split shared dishes among the people who ate them (Itemizing Diner)

> When a dish was shared by only some of us, I want to divide its cost among only the people who ate it, so I can make sure nobody pays for food they did not have.

### J-5 Ask for money in a way people trust (Bill Payer)

> When I ask friends to pay me back, I want to give them a breakdown they can check for themselves, so I can get paid back without arguments about the amount.

### J-6 Collect cash without small change (Bill Payer)

> When people are paying me back in cash, I want to ask for amounts that are easy to hand over, so I can collect the money without anyone dealing with coins and small change.
